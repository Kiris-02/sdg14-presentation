</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-4">
  <TargetCard dimension="Target" title="Target 14.3 Aim & Scope">
    <p><strong class="text-cyan-300">UN Mandate:</strong> "Minimize and address the impacts of ocean acidification, including through enhanced scientific cooperation at all levels."</p>
    <ul class="list-disc list-inside space-y-1 mt-2 text-slate-300">
      <li><strong>Chemical Driver:</strong> Ocean has absorbed ~30% of anthropogenic $CO_2$, causing seawater $pH$ to drop by <strong>0.1 units</strong> since the Industrial Revolution—a <strong>26% increase</strong> in acidity ($\text{H}^+$ ions).</li>
      <li><strong>Ecological Threat:</strong> Depletes carbonate ions ($CO_3^{2-}$), threatening calcifying organisms (pteropods, shellfish, corals) by lowering aragonite saturation ($\Omega_{arag}$).</li>
      <li><strong>Core Focus:</strong> Scientific standardization, international open-data exchange, and capacity building.</li>
    </ul>
  </TargetCard>

  <TargetCard dimension="Indicator" title="Official Indicator 14.3.1">
    <p><strong class="text-emerald-300">Indicator 14.3.1:</strong> "Average marine acidity ($pH$) measured at agreed suite of representative sampling stations."</p>
    <div class="space-y-2 mt-2">
      <div class="p-2 rounded bg-slate-800/80 border border-slate-700">
        <span class="font-bold text-emerald-400 text-xs block">Tier II UN Classification</span>
        <span class="text-[11px] text-slate-300">Indicator has an established methodology overseen by IOC-UNESCO, but data coverage is expanding across ocean basins.</span>
      </div>
      <div class="p-2 rounded bg-slate-800/80 border border-slate-700">
        <span class="font-bold text-emerald-400 text-xs block">Carbonate Parameter Pairing</span>
        <span class="text-[11px] text-slate-300">Requires reporting at least 2 of 4 parameters: $pH$, Total Alkalinity ($TA$), Dissolved Inorganic Carbon ($DIC$), and $pCO_2$.</span>
      </div>
    </div>
  </TargetCard>
</div>
